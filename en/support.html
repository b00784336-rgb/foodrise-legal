Support

A question, a problem, or a request about your data? Write to us: foodrise@yahoo.com. We usually reply within a few business days.

Cancel my subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Foodrise → Cancel. Deleting the app or your Foodrise account does not cancel the subscription.

Refunds

Purchases go through the App Store. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. See also our Withdrawal and refunds page.

Restore purchases

In the app: Profile → “Restore purchases”, with the same Apple ID used for the purchase.

Delete my account and data

In the app: Profile → Account → “Delete my account”. Deletion is immediate and permanent. You can also withdraw your health consent or export your data from Profile.

Emergency

Foodrise is not a medical service. If you are in distress or danger, contact emergency services (112 in Europe) or a health professional.

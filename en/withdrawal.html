Right of withdrawal and refunds

Last updated: 23 October 2026

In short

• You have 14 days to withdraw from a Foodrise Premium purchase (subscription or lifetime access), without giving a reason and at no cost.

• You paid through the App Store: Apple collected your money and Apple issues the refund. The request is therefore made to Apple.

• You can also write to us: we confirm your withdrawal and help you with Apple.

• Cancelling a subscription (so it no longer renews) is not the same as withdrawing (to get a refund).

What the law says

You buy Foodrise Premium at a distance, as a consumer. EU law (Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU, Article 9) and Czech law (Civil Code No. 89/2012 Coll., Sections 1829 et seq.) give you 14 days to withdraw without reason. For digital content or a digital service, the law allows this right to be removed if, before the purchase, you expressly asked for immediate performance and acknowledged losing this right. The app does not ask you for this: we therefore do not apply this exception, and your 14-day right of withdrawal applies in full, to every offer. If this ever changes, we will announce it in advance and it will only concern later purchases.

Start of the 14 days

• Monthly or discounted monthly, with a 7-day free trial: the day of subscription (start of the trial). We also accept a withdrawal requested within 14 days after the first payment.

• Annual or weekly (no trial): the day of purchase.

• Lifetime access (one-time purchase): the day of purchase.

• Automatic renewal: in principle, no new statutory period. You can cancel at any time for the next period, and Apple may grant a refund under its own rules.

If you cancel during the free trial, at least 24 hours before it ends, nothing is charged: there is nothing to refund.

How to withdraw and get a refund

• Through Apple (Apple issues refunds): on the "Report a Problem" page (reportaproblem.apple.com), sign in with your Apple account, choose the Foodrise purchase, then "Request a refund"; or through Apple Support. Apple decides how the request is handled and how the refund is made; we have no access to your request or your payment.

• Through us: tell us about your withdrawal with a clear statement (an email is enough) or with the model form below, at foodrise@yahoo.fr, before the 14-day period ends. We acknowledge receipt by email without delay and tell you how to obtain the refund from Apple. If Apple refuses a withdrawal exercised in time, write to us: we will make sure you are refunded, as required by law.

Tell us the email address of your Foodrise account, the offer purchased, the purchase date and, if possible, the order number on the Apple receipt. Never send us your payment details.

After withdrawal

• Refund: all amounts paid for the purchase must be refunded no later than 14 days after your withdrawal was communicated. The refund is made by Apple, in principle to the payment method used.

• Costs: none.

• Access: Premium access ends once the refund is made; you return to the free version.

• Your data: it remains stored and exportable.

Cancel, withdraw, complain: the difference

• Stop being charged in future: cancel in Settings > your name > Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Premium stays active until the end of the paid period.

• Undo a recent purchase: withdraw within 14 days. Refund by Apple and return to the free version.

• Report that the app does not work as it should: send a complaint to foodrise@yahoo.fr. Fix, or price reduction or refund as provided by law.

• Stop using Foodrise: delete your account and cancel the Apple subscription. Deleting the account does not stop the subscription.

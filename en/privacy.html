This policy explains what data Foodrise processes, why, on what legal basis, and how you stay in control of it.

Who is responsible?

The data controller is Jérôme Jack Anthony Karr, sole trader (podnikající fyzická osoba), company ID (IČO) 30157196, registered in the Czech trade licensing register, seat: Španělská 759/4, 120 00 Prague 2 – Vinohrady, Czech Republic. For any question or to exercise your rights: foodrise@yahoo.fr. No data protection officer is appointed: the publisher handles your requests directly.

What data?

• Account: email address, first name (optional) and password (stored only in encrypted form by the authentication service).

• Tracking: your cravings (type, intensity before/after, trigger, outcome, duration), your check-ins (mood, energy, goal of the day), your missions, lessons and quizzes, your journal (emotion, context, short note), your plan and your anchor (free text), your favorite tools, your challenges and your reminder preferences.

• Progress: your daily path (steps done or skipped), your “For you” preferences (“Not for me”, “Later”, “Already done”), your answers to micro-checks, the tools you finished (with the intensity afterwards if you give it), your end-of-day feeling, your weekly goal, your mission changes and the date of your last visit. This progress data is saved in your account so you can find it again on a new device; it is health data, covered by the same consent, erased if you withdraw your consent or delete your account, and never used for advertising.

• Technical: an account identifier, your time zone, the version and date of your consent, and minimal usage events (the name of the action, never the content of your notes, your journal or your plan).

Foodrise does not access your location, your contacts, your photos, or Health (HealthKit).

Health data and consent

What you note about your relationship with food may reveal information about your health (a special category of data, Article 9 GDPR). We process it only with your explicit consent, freely given when you create your account by ticking a separate box that is unticked by default, and only to make the app work. You can withdraw this consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing: by withdrawing your consent (Profile → Withdraw my health consent, which erases your tracking data, including your progress, and keeps your account), by deleting your account (Profile → Delete my account) or by writing to foodrise@yahoo.fr. Without consent, the tracking features cannot be used.

Why, and on what legal basis?

• Providing the app (account, 90-second exercise, history, statistics, streaks, missions, reminders, sync between your devices): performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

• Processing your tracking data that may reveal your health: your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR).

• Service security, bug fixing and aggregate understanding of which features are used, through minimal usage events without content: the publisher's legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); you can object by writing to us. You can refuse these statistics at any time in Profile → Usage statistics (this also erases those already recorded).

• Accounting and tax obligations: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR); billing of purchases is handled by Apple.

Payment

Payments are processed by Apple. Foodrise receives neither your payment method nor your purchase history; it only reads, on your iPhone, whether your subscription is active. Apple processes this data under its own privacy policy.

What we do not do

No advertising, no tracking across apps, no resale or sharing of your data for commercial purposes, no automated profiling producing decisions about you, no generative artificial intelligence processing your entries.

Who receives your data?

Your data is stored with Supabase (database and authentication), in a data center located in the European Union (Paris). Supabase acts on our behalf as a processor; as the company is established in the United States, any access from outside the European Economic Area is covered by standard contractual clauses. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone: no server sends you notifications. Apple distributes the app and processes purchases. Confirmation and password-reset emails are sent by an email delivery provider acting as a processor. We pass your data to no one else, except where required by law.

For how long?

As long as your account exists. When you delete your account in the app, your data is erased immediately and permanently from the active database. The host's technical backup copies disappear according to its rotation cycle. Emails you send us are kept for the time needed to handle your request, then for at most three years.

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent. You can export your data in JSON format (Profile → Export my data) and delete your account from the app. For any other right: foodrise@yahoo.fr; we reply within one month. You may also lodge a complaint with the Czech Data Protection Authority, Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (ÚOOÚ, Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Prague 7, www.uoou.gov.cz) or with the data protection authority of your country of residence.

Security

Encrypted exchanges (HTTPS), data access limited to your account alone by database-level security rules, encrypted password, session stored in your iPhone's secure keychain. In the event of a data breach likely to affect your rights, we will notify you and inform the competent authority within the legal deadlines.

Intended audience

Foodrise is intended for people aged 15 and over. It is not aimed at children under 15 and does not knowingly collect their data. From the age of 15, you can consent on your own to the processing of your data by this service (Section 7 of Czech Act No. 110/2019 Coll., which applies Article 8 GDPR). If you are under 18, we invite you to talk about it with a trusted adult; if food causes you difficulties, also talk to a healthcare professional. Foodrise is not a medical device. If you are a legal guardian and learn that your daughter or son under 15 uses Foodrise, write to us at foodrise@yahoo.fr: we will delete the account and the data.

Changes

We may update this policy. Each version has a number; if a change is significant, we ask for your agreement again in the app before you continue.

Important

Foodrise is an everyday support tool. It does not replace medical advice or the support of a healthcare professional. If you are going through a difficult time with food, talk to a professional. In case of danger or distress, call 112 (emergencies, throughout the European Union), 155 (medical emergencies in the Czech Republic) or 15 (SAMU, in France).

This policy explains what data Foodrise processes, why, on what legal basis, and how you stay in control of it.

Who is responsible?

The data controller is Jérôme Jack Anthony Karr, sole trader (podnikající fyzická osoba), company ID (IČO) 30157196, registered in the Czech trade licensing register, seat: Španělská 759/4, 120 00 Prague 2 – Vinohrady, Czech Republic. For any question or to exercise your rights: foodrise@yahoo.com. No data protection officer is appointed: the publisher handles your requests directly.

What data?

• Account: email address, first name (optional) and password (stored only in encrypted form by the authentication service).

• Budget: your weekly envelope and the spending you enter by hand (amount, category, date, optional note).

• Pauses before buying: the type of purchase, the intensity of your urge to buy (1 to 10), the amount involved and your decision.

• Notes and tracking: your notes (free text), your missions, lessons and quizzes, your favorite tools, your challenges and your reminder preferences.

• Progress: your level and active days, your daily path (steps done or skipped), your “For you” preferences, the goals you pick at the start (for example tracking your spending), your weekly goal and the date of your last visit. This data is saved in your account so you can find it again on a new device; it is covered by the same consent, erased if you withdraw your consent or delete your account, and never used for advertising.

• Technical: an account identifier, your time zone, the version and date of your consent, and minimal usage events (the name of the action, never the content of your notes or entries).

Foodrise processes no health data and no banking data. It is not connected to any bank account and has no access to any transaction: only the spending you note yourself counts. It does not access your location, your contacts or your photo library. If you choose to scan a receipt (optional), the camera or the photo you select is used only to read the total, the date and the shop, directly on your iPhone: the image and the text read are neither saved, nor sent, nor shared. Only what you confirm (amount, category, note, date) is saved.

Consent to budget tracking

To record your budget tracking data (envelope, spending, pauses before buying, notes, progress), we ask for your consent, freely given when you create your account by ticking a separate box that is unticked by default, and only to make the app work. You can withdraw it at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing: by withdrawing your consent (Profile → Withdraw my consent, which erases your tracking data, including your progress, and keeps your account), by deleting your account (Profile → Delete my account) or by writing to foodrise@yahoo.com. Without consent, the tracking features cannot be used.

Why, and on what legal basis?

• Providing the app (account, 90-second pause, history, statistics, active days, missions, reminders, sync between your devices): performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

• Recording your budget tracking data: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

• Service security, bug fixing and aggregate understanding of which features are used, through minimal usage events without content: the publisher's legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); you can object by writing to us. You can refuse these statistics at any time in Profile → Usage statistics (this also erases those already recorded).

• Accounting and tax obligations: legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR); billing of purchases is handled by Apple.

Payment

Payments are processed by Apple. To verify your purchase and know whether your subscription is active, Foodrise uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which acts on our behalf as a processor. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier, not linked to your Foodrise account or your email, the purchase receipt provided by Apple, your IP address and technical information about your device. Foodrise receives neither your payment method nor your name from Apple. Apple processes its own data under its own privacy policy.

What we do not do

No advertising, no tracking across apps, no resale or sharing of your data for commercial purposes, no connection to your bank account or access to your transactions, no automated profiling producing decisions about you, no generative artificial intelligence processing your entries.

Who receives your data?

Your data is stored with Supabase (database and authentication), in a data center located in the European Union (Paris). Supabase acts on our behalf as a processor; as the company is established in the United States, any access from outside the European Economic Area is covered by standard contractual clauses. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone: no server sends you notifications. Apple distributes the app and processes purchases; RevenueCat, Inc. (United States) verifies purchases and subscription status as a processor, with transfers outside the European Economic Area covered by standard contractual clauses. Confirmation and password-reset emails are sent by an email delivery provider acting as a processor. We pass your data to no one else, except where required by law.

For how long?

As long as your account exists. When you delete your account in the app, your data is erased immediately and permanently from the active database. The host's technical backup copies disappear according to its rotation cycle. Emails you send us are kept for the time needed to handle your request, then for at most three years.

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent. You can export your data in JSON format (Profile → Export my data) and delete your account from the app. For any other right: foodrise@yahoo.com; we reply within one month. You may also lodge a complaint with the Czech Data Protection Authority, Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (ÚOOÚ, Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Prague 7, www.uoou.gov.cz) or with the data protection authority of your country of residence.

Security

Encrypted exchanges (HTTPS), data access limited to your account alone by database-level security rules, encrypted password, session stored in your iPhone's secure keychain. In the event of a data breach likely to affect your rights, we will notify you and inform the competent authority within the legal deadlines.

Intended audience

Foodrise is intended for people aged 15 and over. It is not aimed at children under 15 and does not knowingly collect their data. From the age of 15, you can consent on your own to the processing of your data by this service (Section 7 of Czech Act No. 110/2019 Coll., which applies Article 8 GDPR). If you are under 18, we invite you to talk about it with a trusted adult. If you are a legal guardian and learn that your daughter or son under 15 uses Foodrise, write to us at foodrise@yahoo.com: we will delete the account and the data.

Changes

We may update this policy. Each version has a number; if a change is significant, we ask for your agreement again in the app before you continue.

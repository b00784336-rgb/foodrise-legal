A budget tool, not financial advice

Foodrise helps you keep control of your food budget: note your spending, pause before a purchase and see what is left in your weekly envelope. It is a personal tracking and reflection tool. Foodrise is not an investment advisory service, not a banking or payment service, not a credit institution, and it provides no financial, tax, legal or accounting advice.

No investment, credit or savings advice

Lessons, the coach, missions and audio give general guidance about everyday shopping habits. They do not replace the advice of a qualified professional. Foodrise recommends no investment, loan, savings product or financial product, and is not connected to any bank account: it has no access to your accounts or transactions.

No promise of savings

Foodrise promises no amount of savings and no result: figures shown come only from what you entered yourself, and everyone moves at their own pace. Buying is never a failure in Foodrise: it is information, and the decision is always yours.

If money becomes a source of difficulty

If your budget causes you stress or difficulties (debts, unpaid bills, difficulty making ends meet), talk to someone you trust or to a budget counselling or social support service in your country. Foodrise can support you day to day, but not instead of professional help.

In an emergency

Foodrise is not an emergency service: nobody reads your entries and the app does not alert anyone.

If your life or someone else's is in danger, call 112, the European emergency number, free and valid throughout the European Union.

You can also call medical emergency services directly: 155 in the Czech Republic, 15 (SAMU) in France; elsewhere, the local emergency number.

If you have suicidal thoughts, call 112 or a prevention line: in France, 3114; in the Czech Republic, the psychological first-aid line 116 123.

Staying calm in the app

• Calm mode (Profile): turns off animated XP gains, celebrations, levels, the sun and encouragement notes.

• Reminders: off by default, at most 2 per day, only those you turn on; you can turn them all off in Profile. Discreet notifications show only the app name on the lock screen.

• Active days are not a test: a day without Foodrise does not erase what you have learned.

• You can stop reminders, export your data or delete your account at any time.

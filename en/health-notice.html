Important health and safety notice

Last updated: 24 October 2026

A wellbeing tool, not care

Foodrise helps you pause when a craving to eat arrives, better understand your triggers and keep the choice. It is not a medical device. Foodrise makes no diagnosis and does not treat, prevent or cure any disease or disorder.

No medical or dietary advice

Lessons, the coach, missions and audio give general guidance. They do not replace the advice of a doctor, psychologist or dietitian. Foodrise is not a diet and promises no results: everyone moves at their own pace.

No weight, no calories

Foodrise tracks neither your weight nor calories, and will never ask you to. Eating is never a failure in Foodrise: it is information.

If food becomes a source of suffering

Talk to a healthcare professional (your GP, for example) if:

• you often feel you lose control when you eat;

• you eat large amounts and feel bad afterwards, or you compensate (vomiting, fasting, excessive exercise, medication);

• you strongly restrict what you eat, or your thoughts are taken over by food, weight or your body;

• your health, sleep, work or relationships suffer.

These situations may be signs of an eating disorder. Eating disorders can be treated, and talking early helps. Foodrise can support you day to day, but not instead of professional care.

In an emergency

Foodrise is not an emergency service: nobody reads your entries and the app does not alert anyone.

If your life or someone else's is in danger, call 112, the European emergency number, free and valid throughout the European Union.

You can also call medical emergency services directly: 155 in the Czech Republic, 15 (SAMU) in France; elsewhere, the local emergency number.

If you have suicidal thoughts, call 112 or a prevention line: in France, 3114; in the Czech Republic, the psychological first-aid line 116 123.

Looking after yourself in the app

• Calm mode (Profile): turns off animated XP gains, celebrations, the streak, levels, the sun and encouragement notes.

• Reminders: off by default, at most 2 per day, only those you turn on; you can turn them all off in Profile. Discreet notifications show only the app name on the lock screen.

• Your streak is not a test: a day without Foodrise does not erase what you have learned.

• You can stop reminders, export your data or delete your account at any time.
